# The Good Press

**Good news, shaped by you.**

The Good Press is a phone-first news reader with the familiar swipe interaction of a dating app and the visual character of a printed newspaper.

[Try The Good Press](https://rkhan77.github.io/The-Good-Press/)

## Application features

### Swipeable news feed

- Swipe right for more stories like a card, or left for fewer.
- Use the large action buttons or keyboard arrow keys instead of swiping.
- Undo the most recent choice.
- New readers receive a short visual guide to the More and Less actions.

### Good-news story cards

- Newspaper-inspired design with article title, published date, reading time, category, and a concise story introduction.
- Tap a card to read the full article in a scrollable reader.
- A discussion marker highlights stories with public reaction or community conversation.
- Small editorial notes keep each card warm and human.

### Personal agenda

- Choose from the unique categories currently available in the edition.
- Ten right swipes in a category automatically make it a preferred topic.
- Your agenda shows every available topic, preference strength, and categories to avoid.
- Preferred topics receive the newest unseen stories first.

### Guest and signed-in reading

- Guests can explore a starter edition.
- Signed-in readers unlock live, recent updates and a saved personal agenda.
- Email/password and Google sign-in are available.
- A new reader is welcomed by The newsroom: Alex, Maya, Jim, and Sarah.

### Settings and appearance

- Signed-in account status and agenda choices are available in Settings.
- Light and dark colour modes are available.
- Readers can sign out from the bottom of Settings.

### Responsive and accessible

- Designed for one-handed phone reading and adapted for desktop.
- Clear icon labels, keyboard navigation, escape-to-close panels, and reduced-motion support.
- Readable type and strong contrast in light and dark modes.
