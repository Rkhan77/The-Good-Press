The Good Press

A more hopeful way to stay informed.

The Good Press brings together constructive stories of progress, care and practical change. Sign up to unlock the most recent updates from the past seven days and build a personal agenda around the good news you care about. Each choice helps shape a calmer, more positive edition; guests can explore a small starter edition.

The newsroom

Meet the tiny press gang.

Alex does source work: a cheerful internet ferret who returns with reputable links and the occasional biscuit crumb.

Maya checks the public temperature, politely separating “this rules” from the comment-section goblins yelling at clouds.

Jim turns useful things getting done into punchlines. He insists this is journalism; his stapler has been asked not to comment.
